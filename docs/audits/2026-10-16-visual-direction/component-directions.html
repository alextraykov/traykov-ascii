    Component directions

    Every component on traykov.cc, drawn three ways side by side. The three directions share the same marks, the same density scale and the same clock. They differ in how much of the grid shows, how dense the marks are and how motion reads.

    • A · Ruled paperA quiet editorial page. Hairlines and type do the work; cells appear only where they mean something. Motion is one snap.
    • B · Cell gridThe 8 px lattice made visible. Rules, markers and meters are rows of counted cells. Motion counts in steps on the clock.
    • C · Printed ditherA coarse 12 px dither as the material. Fills, rules and entrances are visibly dotted. Motion is a dissolve held for a beat.

    Hover, press Tab and click inside each column. Switch theme, motion and view in the bar above; Replay runs a component's entrance again. Choose a direction under each component. Your picks stay in this browser and export as plain text at the end of the page.
