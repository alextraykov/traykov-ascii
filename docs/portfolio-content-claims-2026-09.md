## Excluded public claims

- Pave absolute users, signups, retention, revenue, pricing, customer identities, internal links, ticket identifiers, Plan approval ratios, Direct Edit save ratios, restore-success conclusions, and notification-adoption conclusions.
- Pipelines claims that an unaccepted AI suggestion was edited, that builders migrated between features, that opening a generated pipeline proved a repair or successful automation, or that an agent replacement has a future outcome.
- Connection Central raw event volume, a long-window navigation funnel as a usability result, revenue attribution, and claims that the redesigned IA prevented mistakes.
